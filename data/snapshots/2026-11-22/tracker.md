## AudaCity: Fictional crypto brand AudaCity and DacityCoin appears on a plane in the launch trailer

- id: ing-audacity-dacitycoin-plane
- category: ingame
- status: confirmed
- date: 2026-10-15
- location: In-game (Leonida)
- source_name: Decrypt
- source_url: https://decrypt.co/381122/gta-6-trailer-features-meme-coin-and-solana-copycat-soars-2900
- added: 2026-11-14
- updated: 2026-11-14

The GTA VI launch trailer shows a plane reading 'DacityCoin AudaCity', a fictional meme-coin brand. Decrypt says leaked footage had Lucia skydiving in an AudaCity parachute, and the GTA wiki describes the stunt as advertising the brand and the coin. A real Solana token of the same name rose sharply after the trailer. Rockstar did not respond to Decrypt about it.
